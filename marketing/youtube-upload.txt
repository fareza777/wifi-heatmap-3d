WiFi Heatmap 3D : Signal Map | See Your Wi-Fi Coverage

DESCRIPTION
Make your Wi-Fi visible. Walk through your space, map signal measurements in 3D and 2D, and discover where your connection needs attention.

WiFi Heatmap 3D : Signal Map brings room surveys and network tools together: live signal trends, channel analysis, ping and speed tests, local network checks, saved surveys and exportable reports.

The opening shows a real in-app 3D result. Other survey examples use illustrative sample data or clearly labeled concept graphics. AR walking scans require an ARCore-compatible Android device. Results depend on the device and environment; the app does not boost radio power.

English and Indonesian interface. Free with ads and an optional one-time purchase to remove in-app ads.
